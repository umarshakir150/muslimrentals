## Open questions

- Has the roommate-profiles feature itself (data model, matching, visibility rules) been scoped and reviewed by Trust & Safety yet, or is this report-feature request premature relative to that?
- What data will a roommate profile actually store (bio, preferences, photos, location) — this determines the privacy-law analysis for reports referencing that data.
- Should reported users be notified that a report exists against their profile, and can they contest it? (Affects both fairness/due-process posture and privacy-law analysis.)
- What retention period is proposed for report records naming a specific individual, given no account/data deletion path exists platform-wide today?

## Recommendation

Do not scope the "report roommate profiles" feature in isolation. Send this back to Supervisor/Engineering Lead as part of a full roommate-profiles feature intake: (1) roommate profile data model + Trust & Safety review are prerequisites, not parallel work; (2) the report mechanism should extend the existing listing-Report pattern to a person target (fixed abuse-focused reason taxonomy, mandatory human moderator review, no auto-action) rather than invent a new flow; (3) before this ships, get qualified counsel input on retention of person-linked report data given the platform's still-missing account/data-deletion path, and get founder-approved updates to Terms/Safety/Privacy pages so they reflect the new feature. Flag to Supervisor that this task cannot proceed to implementation until the underlying roommate-profile feature itself has gone through Trust & Safety and Legal review, per company/users.md.
